Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-08-01
  Features:
    - Panel beside the train stop GUI, showing two counts vanilla cannot.
    - "Trains with this stop": every schedule whose interrupts name this station at all, as a
      destination, as something to wait on, or as their trigger. One row per train group.
    - "Trains waiting for this stop": trains that want the stop right now, either sent here by
      an interrupt or held elsewhere until it has room. Trains already parked here are excluded.
    - Parametrised station names are matched, so drop-offs named only by wildcard are found. A
      generic interrupt counts only where the group shows evidence of hauling that item.
    - Click a row to open the train.
